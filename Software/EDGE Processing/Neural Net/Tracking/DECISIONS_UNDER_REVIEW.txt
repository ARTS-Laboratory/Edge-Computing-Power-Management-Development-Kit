# PATCH Neural Network — Decisions Under Review

Methodological choices that require literature support, validation, or further investigation before being finalized.

---

## R001 — Window Duration

**Current:** 15 seconds / 750 samples.

**Review:** Determine an appropriate PPG window duration for HR estimation.

---

## R002 — Window Hop

**Current:** 1 second / 50 samples.

**Review:** Determine an appropriate overlap/hop between windows.

---

## R003 — HR Window Label

**Current:** Mean valid Actiheart HR within the window.

**Review:** Mean vs. median vs. other reference-label methods.

---

## R004 — Minimum Reference Coverage

**Current:** At least 8 valid Actiheart samples per 15-second window.

**Review:** Current threshold is arbitrary and requires justification.

---

## R005 — Low Nonzero Actiheart HR

**Previous:** HR below 40 BPM was excluded.

**Concern:** Low HR can be physiologically valid.

**Review:** Determine reference-quality criteria without an arbitrary BPM cutoff.

---

## R006 — PPG Normalization

**Current:** Per-window z-normalization.

**Review:** Determine appropriate normalization for raw PPG.

---

## R007 — CNN Architecture

**Current:** Two Conv1D layers followed by a dense regression layer.

**Review:** Kernel sizes, filters, pooling, and model size require justification/validation.

---

## R008 — Training Loss

**Current:** MSE.

**Review:** Compare with MAE, Huber, or literature-supported alternatives.

---

## R009 — Optimizer / Learning Rate

**Current:** Adam, LR = 0.001.

**Review:** Determine appropriate optimizer and learning rate.

---

## R010 — Batch Size

**Current:** 128.

**Review:** Requires validation or justification.

---

## R011 — Early Stopping

**Current:** Validation MAE, patience = 10.

**Review:** Validate stopping criterion and patience.

---

## R012 — Accelerometer Integration

**Current Findings:** Adding raw XYZ as direct predictive inputs reduced held-out performance in both Dense and CNN models. A separate CNN accelerometer branch improved over single-stream CNN fusion but still did not outperform PPG-only.

**Review:** Investigate whether accelerometry is better used as a motion or PPG-quality indicator rather than as a direct HR-predictive input.
---

## R013 — Motion Metric

**Current:** Standard deviation of XYZ acceleration magnitude.

**Review:** Find a justified measure of motion intensity.

---

## R014 — Motion Bins

**Current:** Test-set 33rd/67th percentiles.

**Review:** Determine whether motion should be continuous or use justified thresholds.

---

## R015 — Final Dataset Split

**Current:** 8 train / 2 validation / 2 test recordings.

**Review:** Establish a final evaluation split after model development.

---

## R016 — Overlapping Window Independence

**Current:** 15-second windows use a 1-second hop, producing 14 seconds of overlap.

**Review:** Determine how overlapping validation/test windows should be treated when reporting statistical performance.

---

## R017 — Reference Rejection and Window Gaps

**Current:** Windows failing reference-HR criteria are excluded.

**Concern:** A short period of invalid reference data may reject multiple overlapping windows.

**Review:** Determine an objective reference-quality and window-rejection method.

---

## R018 — Temporal Sequence Modeling

**Current:** Dense and CNN models treat each 15-second window independently. Training windows are shuffled.

**Review:** Determine whether HR estimation should explicitly use information from previous windows.

**Candidates:** LSTM, GRU, TCN, or other sequence-aware methods.

---

## R019 — Embedded NN Inference Schedule

**Current:** Training uses 15-second windows with a 1-second hop.

**Review:** Determine the final NN processing/update schedule under PATCH memory and compute constraints.

---

## R020 — Neural Network Type

**Current Finding:** PPG-only CNN outperformed the PPG-only Dense NN.

**Review:** Determine whether CNN or another time-series architecture is most appropriate for PATCH.
